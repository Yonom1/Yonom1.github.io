This NJU-3DV onboarding project explores geometry supervision and temporal consistency for sparse-view reconstruction in the Pointrix framework.

I extended the renderer to produce differentiable optical flow, introduced scale-and-shift-invariant monocular-depth supervision with edge-aware weighting, and rebuilt camera indexing so temporal neighbors remain valid across randomized train/validation splits.
