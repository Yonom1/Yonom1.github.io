NJUIS-Wiki is an unofficial knowledge base created and maintained by students of the School of Intelligence Science and Technology at Nanjing University. It turns experience scattered across cohorts into a searchable, evolving, and reusable public resource.

## Why I contribute

Curriculum details, course choices, and practical advice are often scattered across group chats and personal notes, then lost as cohorts graduate. NJUIS-Wiki preserves these experiences so future students can spend less time rediscovering the same information and can continue improving it together.

## My contributions

I am **one of the project's original contributors**, with responsibilities in:

- **Curriculum plans:** organizing and maintaining material that helps students understand the curriculum structure and possible study paths;
- **Course guides:** documenting course information and experience to provide practical references for future students.

## Progress

NJUIS-Wiki received its first public introduction in March 2026. Students across different cohorts continue to maintain sections covering the school, study and graduate pathways, research and competitions, internships and careers, campus life, and more.
