NJUSZ-Canteen Simulator is a Qt6 team project for the Programming Practice course at Nanjing University. Players manage a campus canteen and respond to events as its new manager.

## My contribution

I worked on early planning, video editing, and part of the visual design. The project received a course score of **94.2**.

Team: Liu Xiaorui (planning), Peng Jin (art), and Ye Jin and Chen Bojun (development).
